# P0101.004 - Registration: Registrant Email

**As a** cyber threat intelligence analyst  
**I want** to identify exposed registrant email addresses on a malicious domain  
**So that** I can find other domains registered with the same email.

## Acceptance Criteria
- System extracts the registrant email from RDAP/Whois
- Flags emails that are not privacy-protected
- Treats the email as a high-value pivot for infrastructure clustering

## Why This Pivot Matters
Exposed registrant emails are rare today due to privacy services and are extremely valuable for linking multiple domains to the same threat actor.
